Pacefy

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 12, 2026

Pacefy (the "Service") values your privacy and complies with applicable laws.

This English text is a translation provided for your convenience. The Korean version is the official version, and it prevails if the two differ.

1. Personal information we collect

  • Required: email address (member identification and authentication)
  • Created through use of the Service: location information (GPS routes), activity records (distance, time, pace, step count), meal records (time of meal, meal type, the meal description you enter yourself, estimated carbohydrate range), profile (nickname, bio, profile image), community posts, direct messages and club chat messages
  • Collected automatically: device information, access logs

2. Purposes of collection and use

  • Sign-up and identity verification
  • Storing walking and running records and providing statistics
  • Confirming and recording post-meal walks
  • Providing community, challenge, and ranking features
  • Delivering and retaining messages between users (direct messages and club chat)
  • Issuing ranking rewards (mobile coupons) and managing issuance records
  • Improving the Service and responding to inquiries

3. Handling of location information

  • Location information is used only for the purpose of recording activity routes.
  • It is collected only while an activity is in progress, and the route is stored on your device after the activity ends.
  • So that your records can be restored when you change devices or reinstall the app, a simplified copy of the route (reduced to at most 300 coordinates with decimal places truncated) is also stored on our server, linked only to your own account. No one other than you can access it, and it is destroyed when you delete your account.
  • You can withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings (routes cannot be recorded after withdrawal).

3-1. Handling of motion and fitness (step count) data

  • The Service reads step counts from your device's motion and fitness sensors in order to confirm and record post-meal walks.
  • Only the step count is read; other health data such as heart rate, and location, are not collected through this channel.
  • The queried window is at most 90 minutes from the time a meal is recorded; no other period is queried.
  • Step counts are stored and transmitted as part of your activity records.
  • You can withdraw motion and fitness permission at any time in your device settings (step counts cannot be recorded after withdrawal).

3-2. Meal description input and carbohydrate estimation

  • If you enter a one-line description of your meal, the Service sends that sentence to the AI service of Anthropic PBC in order to estimate an approximate range of carbohydrate intake, which is used to calculate a recommended walking time.
  • This input is optional, and you can use every feature of the Service without entering it.
  • Only the sentence you enter is transmitted; your email address, profile, location information, and activity records are not transmitted.
  • The sentence you enter and the estimated result are stored on your device as part of your meal records, and are also stored on our server linked only to your own account so that they can be restored when you change devices or reinstall the app. No one other than you can access them, and they are destroyed when you delete your account.
  • The Service stores this sentence solely for the purpose of keeping your own records, and does not use it for any other purpose such as advertising, profiling, or AI training.
  • Whether an estimation took place (success or failure) is recorded separately, for usage limits and quality improvement.
  • Estimated results are for reference only and are not a medical diagnosis or medical advice. They may differ from your actual intake.

3-3. Handling of messages

  • Direct messages and club chat messages are stored on our server so that they can be delivered to the recipient and reviewed again by you.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, and may be reviewed where required by applicable law, such as for handling reports or disputes.
  • You may use the block feature, and messages you have sent are destroyed when you delete your account.

3-4. Handling of reward coupons

  • Mobile coupons issued as ranking rewards are issued through Giftishow Biz, operated by KT Alpha Co., Ltd.
  • No personal information (such as email address or phone number) is passed on for coupon issuance; the Service receives the issued coupon number and displays it in the in-app coupon box.
  • A coupon number is shown only to the person who received it.

4. Provision to third parties and entrustment of processing

  • The Service entrusts the storage and authentication of user data to Supabase Inc. (Seoul, Republic of Korea region).
  • The Service entrusts the carbohydrate estimation for meal description input (3-2) to Anthropic PBC.
  • The Service entrusts the issuance of reward coupons (3-4) to KT Alpha Co., Ltd.
  • We do not provide personal information to third parties for purposes other than the entrusted ones.

5. Transfer of personal information overseas

  • Service data is stored in the Seoul, Republic of Korea region of Supabase Inc.'s cloud infrastructure, and is not transferred overseas in the course of storage or authentication.
  • However, if you enter a meal description, it is transferred overseas as follows for carbohydrate estimation.
  • Recipient: Anthropic PBC
  • Country and location of transfer: cloud data centers located in the United States
  • Items transferred: the meal description sentence you enter yourself
  • Purpose of transfer: estimating carbohydrate intake (to calculate a recommended walking time)
  • Time of transfer: only when you enter a meal description and request a calculation
  • Retention and use period: the period necessary for processing the estimate
  • You may refuse the overseas transfer; in that case, sign-up and use of the Service may be restricted.

6. Retention and destruction

  • Member information (email address, profile): destroyed without delay when you delete your account
  • Activity records, location information (including route copies), meal records, posts, and messages: destroyed when you delete your account (destroyed immediately when you delete an item yourself)
  • Access logs and device information: destroyed three months after collection
  • Where retention is required by applicable law, the information is stored separately for that period and then destroyed.

7. Your rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data directly in the app at [Settings > Delete account].

8. Personal information of children under 14

  • The Service does not collect the personal information of children under the age of 14, and persons under 14 cannot register as members.

9. Privacy officer

  • Privacy officer: the representative of TripleA Lab
  • Contact: a3lab01.create@gmail.com

10. Duty of notification

  • We will announce any change to this policy through an in-app notice or through this screen.
